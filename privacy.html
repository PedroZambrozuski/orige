ORIGE - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

ORIGE is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels NOCTE THEORY and EFFUGE to upload and schedule videos to their own channels.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization, ORIGE uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, publication time and playlists on the owner's channel. It does not read, collect or store data about other users or viewers.

Storage

The authorization token is stored only on the owner's computer or private server and is never shared. No data is sold or transferred to third parties.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google policies

ORIGE uses YouTube API Services and follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
